Reading and Written Response #3

The graphic novel “Motherhood in Crisis” communicates how dangerous it is to be pregnant in Sierra Leone. Before I already knew that childbirth is not easy and in many cases is complicated but after reading the novel I realized that I hadn't thought about pregnancy in other parts of the world. The story shows that about one in ten women unfortunately died during childbirth, which is shocking. I have always thought about many things that make us different but never about the way we are born. Memunatu, Heela, Kadiatu, and Tette share very powerful stories about how they face different problems with strength. Memunaru’s story shows how hard it is to get medical help for people like her who live far away from the hospital. I made a comparison and I realized that every person experiences this issue but in her country it is more complicated to access healthcare. Heela’s story is also similar in a sad way. She’s a young girl and her future changes because of pregnancy. A pregnancy at an early age can take away opportunities and education from these girls. This situation makes me create a connection with issues like gender inequality. It’s frustrating how a man can get a woman pregnant and nothing bad happens in their life, they don't face consequences. While pregnant girls do not get the chance to make their own decisions and future. Kadiatu shares an emotional story about losing her child. Her story expresses the fear and sadness that many mothers around the world experience. I’m not a mom but I can empathize with the pain of losing someone important in their life. Her story helps my knowledge about the emotional impact on many families and communities. Tetteh’s story is a little different because it connects motherhood with the correctional center system. It shows how these systems can punish women in ways that ignore their needs. Sometimes the system is so unfair with people, especially to women. Overall, the graphic novel makes experiences feel more real and easier to understand through the images and also personal stories. Instead of just reading articles, I could see and imagine their struggles and emotions on their faces making the message more impactful. Another important point is how diseases like COVID-19 have made these situations more difficult. These crises just added more weakness to the healthcare system making countries more vulnerable. In conclusion, this graphic novel is an eye opening to the dangers that mothers experience in places with poor healthcare. It also showed the strength that women have when they go through these challenges. The stories made me think about the importance of gender equality and healthcare systems around the world. After reading this graphic novel, I believe people should also read stories like this to understand how others face different realities.
